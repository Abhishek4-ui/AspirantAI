# Class 10th & Class 12th Board Preparation Guide

## 1. Class 10th Board Hub
- **Supported Boards**: Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB), ICSE, UP Board.
- **Core Subjects**:
  - Mathematics Standard (Real Numbers, Polynomials, Linear Equations, Trigonometry).
  - Science (Chemical Reactions, Life Processes, Light Reflection & Refraction).
  - Social Science, English, Hindi.
- **Features**: NCERT chapter notes, formula cheat sheets, definitions, diagrams, and chapter MCQs.

## 2. Class 12th Senior Secondary Hub
- **Streams Supported**:
  - **Science (PCM)**: Physics (Electrostatics, Current Electricity, Magnetism), Chemistry (Solutions, Electrochemistry, Organic Mechanisms), Mathematics (Calculus, Vectors, Probability).
  - **Science (PCB)**: Physics, Chemistry, Biology (Genetics, Evolution, Human Reproduction, Biotechnology).
  - **Commerce**: Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics, Applied Mathematics.
  - **Arts / Humanities**: History, Political Science, Geography, Sociology, Economics.
- **Features**: Worked examples, derivation steps, numerical practice sheets, and previous year board papers.
